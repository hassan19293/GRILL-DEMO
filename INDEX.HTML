Our Story

Born from a love of fire, craft and honest ingredients.

The Grill House began with a single question: what happens when you strip a steakhouse back to what matters? No shortcuts, no gimmicks — just fire, time, and ingredients treated with respect.

Today, that same philosophy shapes every plate that leaves our kitchen.
